Lasagna

Description

This classic lasagna recipe features rich layers of savory meat sauce, tender pasta sheets, and a creamy mixture of ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheeses.

It is the ultimate comfort food, perfect for family dinners or meal prep. Baking it slowly allows all the flavors to meld together into a delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 package lasagna noodles
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a skillet over medium heat, cook and brown the ground beef. Drain excess fat and mix in the marinara sauce.
  3. In a small bowl, combine ricotta cheese, egg, salt, and pepper.
  4. In a baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce, followed by a layer of noodles, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella. Repeat layers.
  5. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 20 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  6. Let cool for 10–15 minutes before serving.
