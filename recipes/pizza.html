Homemade Pizza

Description

A classic homemade pizza with a crispy crust, rich tomato sauce, melted mozzarella, and fresh basil leaves.

Making pizza dough from scratch is easier than you think and allows you to customize your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 packet active dry yeast
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves and oregano

Steps

  1. Dissolve yeast in warm water and let sit for 5 minutes until frothy.
  2. Mix flour, olive oil, and yeast mixture to form a dough ball. Knead for 5 minutes and let rise for 30 minutes.
  3. Preheat oven to 475°F (245°C).
  4. Roll out the dough onto a baking sheet, spread pizza sauce, and top with cheese and seasonings.
  5. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the crust is golden brown and cheese is melted.
