Chicken Noodle Soup

Description

A comforting chicken noodle soup packed with tender chicken, vegetables, and egg noodles in a flavorful broth.

Perfect for cold days or when you are feeling under the weather, this soup comes together quickly in one pot.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery stalks, sliced
  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cups cooked shredded chicken
  • 1 1/2 cups egg noodles
  • Salt, pepper, and fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion, carrots, and celery; cook until softened.
  2. Pour in chicken broth and bring to a boil.
  3. Add egg noodles and simmer for 8–10 minutes until tender.
  4. Stir in cooked chicken, salt, pepper, and chopped parsley. Heat through for 2 minutes before serving.
